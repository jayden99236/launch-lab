How difficulty adapts

Each station keeps a skill rating from 1 to 10. Problems are pitched close to it, aiming for about 7 right out of 10, which is where people learn fastest.

Beating a hard problem raises your rating a lot; missing an easy one lowers it. Two misses in a row bring an easier problem, and three correct answers in a row bring a stretch problem.

Missed problems go into your review queue. Get one right later and it leaves the queue. Using a hint caps that problem's credit at 60%.
